01 What information do we collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

Pharmacue does not currently require you to create an account or log in. The App is designed to work without collecting personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number, unless you choose to contact us directly (for example, by emailing us for support).

Information collected automatically

When you use the App, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device data: device model, manufacturer, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, and IP address, used to keep the App secure, operational, and for internal troubleshooting and analytics.
  • Usage data: information about how you interact with the App, such as which lessons or exercises you complete, used to improve the learning experience.
  • Voice and speech data: when you use the App's pronunciation and interpretation practice features, audio of your speech is captured and processed in order to provide speech-recognition feedback. This audio is processed via on-device or third-party speech-recognition services (see Section 4) and is not used to identify you personally.

02 How do we process your information?

We process the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To deliver and facilitate the App's vocabulary-learning and speech-practice features.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To send you administrative information about the App, such as updates to this policy.
  • To request feedback and improve the App.
  • To protect the App and its users, including detecting and preventing fraud or abuse.
  • To evaluate and improve the App's features, performance, and content.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

03 When and with whom do we share your information?

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • AI and speech-recognition platforms: audio you record during practice exercises may be sent to third-party AI and speech-recognition platforms (such as Apple's or Google's on-device/cloud speech services) solely to generate pronunciation and interpretation feedback.
  • Business transfers: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

04 Do we offer artificial-intelligence-based products?

Yes. As part of our features, we offer products, features, or tools powered by artificial intelligence, including natural language and speech processing, to score pronunciation, evaluate interpretation accuracy, and provide learning feedback.

How this works: your recorded speech is processed by AI-powered speech-recognition services to generate feedback. We do not use this audio to build a profile of you for advertising, and we take reasonable steps to ensure any third-party AI providers we use handle this data in a manner consistent with this Privacy Policy.

05 How long do we keep your information?

We keep your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (such as for tax, accounting, or other legal requirements). When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your information, we will delete or anonymize it.

06 How do we keep your information safe?

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any information we process. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorized third parties will not be able to defeat our security and improperly collect, access, steal, or modify your information.

07 Do we collect information from minors?

We do not knowingly collect data from or market to children under 18 years of age. By using the App, you represent that you are at least 18 or that you are the parent or guardian of a minor and consent to their use of the App. If we learn that personal information from users under 18 has been collected, we will deactivate the account and take reasonable measures to promptly delete such data. If you become aware of any data we may have collected from children under 18, please contact us at hehuidanmail@gmail.com.

08 What are your privacy rights?

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, which may include the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

The easiest way to exercise your rights is by visiting this data request page, or by contacting us directly at hehuidanmail@gmail.com. We will consider and act upon any request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

09 Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized, so we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals.

10 Do we make updates to this notice?

Yes, we will update this Privacy Policy as necessary to stay compliant with relevant laws and to reflect changes to the App. The updated version will be indicated by a revised "Last updated" date, and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
